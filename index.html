About Me


Hi, my name is Síne. I am an Ireland based creative professional and a recent graduate of South East Technological University (SETU), Carlow with a degree in 'Computing in Interactive Digital Art and Design'.


My degree sits at the centre of technology and aesthetics, specializing in web development, animation, and graphic design and a bit of Game Design. I began my academic journey there at 23, diversifying my technical skillset and honing my artistic intuition. Since then I've spent my time creating my online portfolio and every item within it.
